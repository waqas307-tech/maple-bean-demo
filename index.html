Our story

A coffee shop that feels like a neighbour's kitchen

Maple & Bean started in 2019 with one espresso machine, a borrowed oven and a simple idea: great coffee should never feel rushed or fancy — it should feel like home.

Six years on, we still roast every bean in small batches at the back of the shop, still bake at sunrise, and still know most of our regulars by name — and by order.
